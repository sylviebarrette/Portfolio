Sylvie Barrette

Visual communication rooted in curiosity, structure, and storytelling.

I see design as a way of making sense of ideas, whether that means starting from scratch or shaping something that is already taking form. I am drawn to projects that invite curiosity, ask good questions, and turn complex thinking into clear, engaging visual outcomes.

My work spans graphic design in its many forms, including branding and identity, print and editorial layout, motion, mixed media, UX/UI, and visual storytelling. I enjoy thinking about how ideas translate across different formats, and how design can stay coherent while adapting to context.

I am comfortable working independently and value collaboration when it adds depth and perspective. What motivates me most is work that creates clarity, connection, or meaning, design that feels considered rather than decorative.

Illustration and photography are also part of my process, feeding directly into how I build concepts and visual systems. Having my work recognised through The Crag climbing photo contest and being selected for display at the Powerhouse Ultimo in 2023 gave me the confidence to keep leaning into the creative paths that matter most to me.
