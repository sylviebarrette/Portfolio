Climate Change Conversation

Curator-selected climate posters exhibited at the Powerhouse Museum.

  • Challenge
  • Process
  • Outcome

Project

100% Plant Based

Year

2023

Role

Illustration, Photography

Tools

Illustrator, Photoshop

Outcome

High Distinction

The challenge

Combat the climate fatigue; standard environmental imagery (like melting ice caps) is often ignored because it has become monotonous.

the strategy

I implemented a three-tier typographic hierarchy to manage the flow of information. By using heavy weights for the 'hook' and strategic negative space for the logistics, I created a clear path for the eye. This allowed the poster to function as both a long-distance beacon and a legible source of detail for those who stepped closer.

From Sketch to Scale

I moved from cluttered, data-heavy sketches to a refined three-tier hierarchy. This process was about stripping away visual noise until only the most critical information remained.

Milestone 1: the struggle

Initial drafts relied too heavily on complex photography and literal imagery. This created visual noise that obscured the core event information.

Milestone 2: the breakthrough

I pivoted to a typographic-led approach to regain clarity. I experimented with using photography as a structural element. finally, forming the '00' in '100%' with vegan patties to blend the visual metaphor with the text.

Milestone 3: Final Refinement

The final iteration focuses on a strict grid and high-contrast weights. By stripping away the 'busy' background elements, I ensured the Powerhouse audience could identify the topic and the date in under three seconds.

Recognition & Outcome

This project received a High Distinction and was selected by the museum curator on 2 seperate ooccasions to be displayed at the Powerhouse Museum.
