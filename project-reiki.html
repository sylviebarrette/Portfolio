The Challenge

How do you translate the intangible energy of Reiki into a professional, premium care brand for the equine industry?

Design Approach

The visual language uses fluid lines to symbolise the flow of energy and the intuitive connection between horse and carer. This organic movement is grounded by a palette inspired by the natural environment, ensuring the brand feels both spiritual and deeply rooted in the physical world.
