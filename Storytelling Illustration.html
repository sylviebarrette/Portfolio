The Echo Diary

An Interactive Narrative for Youth Mental Health

Storytelling Illustration

Role

Art Director, Editorial Designer, Photographer & Illustrator

Tools

InDesign, Photoshop, Watercolours

Context

Exploratory Editorial Series

The Challenge

Visually capturing the restorative "feeling" of nature immersion to motivate reader engagement. The goal was to transform a health-based article into a sensory, peaceful experience that avoids clinical clichés.

The Approach

To balance high-impact advocacy with clear information design, I utilised minimalist symbolism and a high-contrast palette to command attention. The series distinguishes between two functions: one poster acts as a bold visual 'hook' featuring a singular slogan, while the second integrates complex event data into a clean, hierarchical layout to drive attendance for the climate talks.

The Strategy

Creating Textures

Analog-Digital Hybrid serves in fusing watercolour washes with photography to create organic "immersion."

Typographic Hierarchy

Dynamic hierarchy and strategic use of varying weights, sizes, and orientations to guide the eye.

Composition

Intentional use of negative space to create a peaceful reading pace and eye-rest architecture.

Creative Evolution

Fusing analog media with digital photography to create a seamless sense of immersion.

Original Capture

Focusing on natural light and human connection with the nature.

Analog Component

Hand-painted watercolour washes to add organic depth.

Digital Manupulation

Digital color-grading and scaling were applied to the original watercolour to align with the photograph’s palette.

Digital Synthesis

Final composite layered in Photoshop for a restorative feel.
