Sentenced to Sleep

Motion Design

Title Sequence: Sentenced to Sleep

Note: This is a speculative conceptual sequence. Names (e.g., Stephen Spielberg) are used for art direction purposes only.

Role

Motion Director / Editor

Tools

Premiere Pro, Illustrator, Photoshop

Context

Speculative Title Sequence (Concept Study)

The Challenge

The objective was to deconstruct a familiar folk narrative and rebuild it for a contemporary viewer. The primary challenge lay in establishing a seamless visual cohesion between imagery and narrative intent, preparing the audience for the specific noir-inspired world they were about to enter.

Design Approach

Motion & Art Direction The visual foundation of the sequence is built on a series of heavily manipulated photographs, utilising surrealist compositing to mirror the narrative's psychological layers. Every element serves as a hidden detail—for instance, a lunar eye where the pupil reveals a hand clutching a phone. To enhance the atmospheric tension, I integrated custom digital illustrations and dynamic typography.

Academic Impact

Following its completion, this project received a High Distinction and was formally adopted by Western Sydney University as the curriculum benchmark for future students, cited for its technical discipline and innovative approach to storytelling.

Storyboard
