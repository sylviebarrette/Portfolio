Behind a Marsk

Photography & Editorial Art Direction

Role

Photographer & Art Director

Tools

Nikon D850, lighting equipment, Capture One, Photoshop

Context

Exploratory Photography Series

The Challenge

The primary challenge was to execute a high-end fashion editorial in a challenging, low-light industrial environment. I needed to balance the safety and logistical constraints of an active road tunnel with the creative demand for 'magazine-standard' lighting and composition.

The Approach

I approached this by Focusing on the intersection of industrial verticality and street-level perspective. By aligning the tunnel's structural ribs with the road's leading lines, I created a geometric frame that centers the subject within the vast concrete environment.

A hybrid lighting strategy: utilising ambient light from the tunnel's aperture as a primary source, supplemented by high-output speedlights to accentuate corrugated textures and "pop" the subject from the industrial backdrop.

The Workflow

Location & Lighting Research

Assessing vertical geometry, leading lines & natural light prior to the photoshoot.

Natural & Artificial Light Testing

Accentuating textures with high-output speedlights.

Editorial Design

Designing a 12-column system for consistent layout and typographic hierarchy.
