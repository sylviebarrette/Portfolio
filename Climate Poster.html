Climate Crisis Awareness

Public Impact Poster

Role

Lead Graphic Designer

Tools

Illustrator

Outcome

High Distinction

The challange

The goal was to create a high-impact visual that required zero explanation by condensing a complex global crisis into a single, shocking metaphor. I had to move away from literal, cluttered imagery to find a visual 'gut-punch'.

The strategy

My strategy was to use a 'gross-out' visual that felt familiar but wrong—the Earth shaped like a bloody steak. By simplifying the art and focusing on the 'Stake/Steak' wordplay, I cut out the fluff so the message was clear the second you looked at it.

From Sketch to Scale

This was the process of how I stripped away elements and concepts to reach a clearer, more powerful visual direction.

Milestone 1: the struggle

My early concepts were too literal and could take too long to interpret. Scenes like the BBQ or the baby eating the earth felt cluttered and required too much time to analyse, which diluted the shock effect.

Milestone 2: the breakthrough

I began cutting the 'meat' of the subject by going toward a punchier wordplay. I experimented with different illustration styles, eventually realising that the more complex the drawing, the less powerful the message became.

Milestone 3: Final Refinement

The final iterations were about testing different layouts to maximize impact. I experimented with scale and placement to ensure the 'Steak' visual and the 'High Stakes' pun worked together perfectly.

Recognition & Outcome

This project received a High Distinction and was selected by the museum curator to be displayed at the Powerhouse Museum.
